GST Compliance Guidelines

Ledgerly Enterprise is built to strictly adhere to the GST rules and regulations mandated by the Government.

1. Invoice Generation

All invoices generated follow the mandated format under Rule 46 of the CGST Rules, 2017, including HSN/SAC codes, correct tax bifurcation (CGST/SGST/IGST), and sequential invoice numbering.

2. GSTR Reports

Our GSTR-1 and GSTR-2 export formats are continuously updated to align with the latest JSON schema requirements of the GST Portal.

3. Data Retention

We provide tools to help you maintain books of accounts for the statutorily required period of 72 months from the due date of furnishing of annual return.
